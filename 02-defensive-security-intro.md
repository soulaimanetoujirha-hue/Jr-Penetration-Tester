## Today's Lesson

Imagine that right now, somewhere, hackers are quietly testing a company's website — secretly exploring ways to break into their systems.

These hackers are sneaky and full of tricks. The people whose job is to notice these hackers, protect the company, and stop the attacks are called defenders.

## Tools

A security dashboard, e.g. **Security Hub**

## Usage

There are three parts: **Alert**, **Investigate**, **Respond**.

When a hacker starts probing admin pages using a computer, the dashboard receives their IP (e.g. `32.122.195.63`), which we can use to investigate — seeing which pages they were looking for, such as admin, login, or backup pages.

Then, depending on the type of attack, we can respond in several ways, such as: blocking the attacker's IP, slowing down their requests, or turning on additional protection.
